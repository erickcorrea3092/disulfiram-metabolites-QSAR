# disulfiram-metabolites-QSAR

For this project antecedents, Disulfiram is a drug used for the treatment of alcoholism related to the inhibition of aldehyde dehydrogenase (ALDH). It is know that the disulfiram metabolites is related to a anti-metastasis activity. The Mean metabolites of disulfiram is Diethyldithiocarbamic acid (DDC), cupper complexes (Cu(DDC)₂), Diethylthiomethylcarbamate (Me-DTC / S-methyl N,N-diethyldithiocarbamate), and active oxidized metabolites (sulfines and sulfones).    Our investigation group focuses to the synthesis of these metabolites and other compounds with structural variations for establishing Structure Activity Relationships (SAR). A QSAR model was created to make predictions of the new compounds.
